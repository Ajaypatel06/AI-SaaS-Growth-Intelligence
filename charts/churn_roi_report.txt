Churn-ROI — Business Impact of Rewrite Adoption
=======================================================

Paying users without Rewrite : 4,768
  Churn rate                  : 28.82%
  MRR already lost to churn    : Rs.2,392,626

Paying users with Rewrite     : 4,203
  Churn rate                  : 18.89%
  MRR already lost to churn    : Rs.1,437,206

Churn-rate gap                : 9.9 percentage points
Subscriptions saveable         : ~473
Estimated MRR recovery         : Rs.838,502 / month
Estimated ARR recovery         : Rs.10,062,023 / year

Business read: if non-Rewrite paying users churned at the same rate as
Rewrite adopters, the business would retain an estimated Rs.10,062,023
in annual recurring revenue. This converts the product recommendation
("make Rewrite the default first action") into a revenue-justified ask.
